Provide, Accept, and Join are answered with an Unimplemented message that echoes the message received, matching Cap'n Proto C++ 1.4.0. The Janet functions `rpc-provide`, `rpc-send-accept`, `rpc-disembargo`, `rpc-share-vat`, and `rpc-provisions` are removed, along with `capnp_rpc_send_provide`, `capnp_rpc_send_accept`, `capnp_rpc_send_disembargo_provide`, `capnp_rpc_pending_provisions`, `capnp_rpc_embargoed_accepts`, and `capnp_rpc_set_vat`.
